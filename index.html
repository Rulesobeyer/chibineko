chibineko

A little 3D cat, remade from oneko.js. Move your mouse (or tap) and it follows. Ignore it for a while and it'll sit down, scratch, wander over to the edge of the screen to claw at it, and eventually nod off. Click it to give it a pet.

Colour

Pick one here, or right-click the cat (long-press on a phone) for the colour ring.

Behaviours

Use it

Paste this into any page:

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Rulesobeyer/chibineko@1/chibineko.min.js"
  data-persist-position></script>
